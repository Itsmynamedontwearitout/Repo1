# Karl's preferences

## Plant care

- **No "toxic" chemicals.** Don't recommend synthetic insecticides, such as systemics (imidacloprid, acetamiprid) or synthetic pyrethroids. Start with physical removal, water and isopropyl alcohol. A mild option like insecticidal soap can be mentioned as optional, with its caveats. (Set 2026-10-09, after Claude's first mealybug answer suggested a systemic.)
- **Happy to throw out a struggling plant.** Don't push long rescue efforts. Give a clear point at which to stop and replace the plant.
- **Happy to do fiddly hands-on work**, such as picking off pests with tweezers and dabbing them with isopropyl.
- **Wants a short, practical plan.** When Karl says that's all for now, record the follow-ups instead of adding more steps.
- **Monitors plants with the Willow app and sensor.** These report growing conditions such as moisture, light and temperature. They can't see pests or disease, so "Willow says it's happy" doesn't mean the plant is healthy. When something looks wrong, ask for close-up photos of the stems, the undersides of leaves and the base of the plant.
